FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it free?

Yes. Download it and use it. No account needed.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not yet. Right now Aether is available for Windows 10 and 11.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. Your chat history is stored on your own computer, but your messages and attachments are sent over the internet to generate replies.

A file I attached wasn't understood.

Check the supported file list above. Very long documents are shortened, and only the first 12 minutes of an audio or video file are transcribed.

How do I uninstall it?

Open Windows Settings, then Apps, then Installed apps. Find Aether AI and choose Uninstall.
