A chat app that stays out of your way.

Aether AI is built for everyday questions, writing, and code, in a clean dark interface that feels at home on Windows.

History stays on your device

Your chat history is stored on your own computer. Replies are generated online, so you need an internet connection.

Attach almost anything

Images, PDFs, Word files, code, audio and video. Drop files anywhere in the window, or paste an image with Ctrl+V.

Three models in one app

Blyx 3.1 for speed, Zylo 4.5 for everyday use, Qira 5.5 for hard problems. Switch from the top bar any time.

Search your history

Find any past chat fast, with conversations grouped by day.

Your own instructions

Tell Aether how to behave once, and it applies to every chat.

Keyboard friendly

Enter to send, Shift+Enter for a new line, Ctrl+N for a new chat.

Download Aether AI

Version 1.1.0 for Windows 10 and 11. Choose whichever mirror works best for you.

Download from MediaFire Download from Mega

Source code and details are on GitHub.

  1. Download Aether AI Setup 1.1.0.exe from either mirror.
  2. Run it. Aether installs in seconds and adds a Start menu entry and desktop shortcut.
  3. Open Aether AI and start chatting.

If Windows shows a warning

  1. SmartScreen often flags new apps from small developers.
  2. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Antivirus flagged it? Unsigned apps sometimes trigger false alarms. The source is public on GitHub, and you can ask anything in the Discord.
